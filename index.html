About

My specialty is where process quality meets data science: building systems, analyses, and automation tools that keep complex manufacturing processes validated, compliant, and continuously improving.

I architected and served as the sole owner of the company-wide Continued Process Verification (CPV) program for the MU Research Reactor, a cGMP manufacturing facility with multiple radiopharmaceutical API product lines. I developed custom VBA-driven statistical analysis and data visualization tools for real-time Statistical Process Control (SPC) monitoring. Before that, I led the QC Microbiology department, driving countless process improvements, including revision of the most commonly performed procedures that reduced associated human errors by 50% and cut operational time and costs by over 30% while maintaining strict cGMP and regulatory compliance.

My background spans the full quality lifecycle — from bench-level analytical testing as a QC Chemist and microbiologist, through method development and validation and department leadership, to enterprise-level process validation, monitoring, and reporting and strategic planning. I hold an M.S. in Data Science and Analytics and am a Six Sigma Black Belt candidate. I am open to remote roles in CPV, process monitoring, and technical writing.
